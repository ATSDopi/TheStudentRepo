# ❓ FAQ — student discounts & verification

Real answers to the questions that come up again and again.

## Verification

**I don't have a `.edu` / school email — can I still get verified?**
Usually yes. SheerID, UNiDAYS and most verifiers accept an **enrollment certificate, student ID card photo, or acceptance letter** instead of a school email. ISIC works everywhere as a fallback. If a specific offer only takes a `.edu`, ask your registrar — many schools issue an address on request.

**SheerID / UNiDAYS rejected me — what now?**
- Check that your name/school **exactly matches** your official enrollment record (no nicknames).
- Use a **recent** document (current term), clearly showing name + school + date.
- Upload a PDF scan, not a blurry photo.
- Retry once — many rejections are automated. Still failing? Contact their support; manual review usually fixes it.

**I'm in high school — what can I actually get?**
More than you'd think: the **GitHub Student Pack** (13+, with student ID), **Hack Club** programs, **Sketch** (16+), **Planet Fitness summer pass** (14-19), **Apple education pricing** (often open to K-12 students/parents), **FT Schools** free FT (16-19), **ISIC/ITY cards**. Many brand discounts are university-only — check each offer's terms.

**My school isn't listed in the verifier's dropdown — am I stuck?**
Pick "school not listed" / manual verification if offered, or email support with proof. Small/new institutions get added all the time.

## Using the offers

**How often do I need to re-verify?**
Typically **once a year** (Spotify, Headspace, SheerID deals) or per subscription period. Put a reminder in your calendar — lapsing = silently paying full price.

**Do student discounts stack with sales/promo codes?**
Almost never. Compare both prices — a Black Friday price sometimes beats the student rate.

**What happens when I graduate?**
Verification lapses on the next re-check — expect to keep your plan until it renews. Download/export anything stored in edu accounts (email, cloud, licenses) **before** graduation; many schools cut access within months. Nice loophole: **UNiDAYS GRADLiFE** keeps giving you discounts for ~3 years after you graduate — switch your account before it expires.

**Is the ISIC card worth paying for?**
If you travel or visit museums abroad — yes. It costs ~$20 and is the only student proof accepted almost everywhere (130+ countries). If you never leave your campus, your regular student card is usually enough.

**Offers that check age only ("under 26") — no student status?**
Correct — youth fares, EU museum freebies and many railcards only need an ID proving your age. Being a student is irrelevant; everyone under the age cap qualifies.

## Money & safety

**Is it worth buying a cheap "shared"/"lifetime" edu account online?**
No — it's a scam vector. Sellers use stolen or recycled `.edu` accounts; they get revoked, and you can lose your data or the account entirely. Every offer in this repo is free to claim legitimately.

**Are "student discount" blogs with big lists trustworthy?**
Be skeptical — many list **expired or invented offers** (e.g. "Netflix student discount" doesn't exist) to rank on Google or push affiliate links. Always confirm on the vendor's official page — that's why every row here links to it.

**Do I need a credit card for "free" student plans?**
Some ask for one at signup (Google AI promos, trials) — set a cancel reminder the same day. Truly card-free offers are marked as such (e.g. Azure for Students).
