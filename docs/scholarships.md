## National aid (start here — easiest money)

Most students qualify for **some** national aid but never apply. See your country in [countries.md](countries.md):

- 🇫🇷 France → CROUS grant (DSE), APL, merit aid
- 🇺🇸 USA → FAFSA (Pell Grant = free money), state grants
- 🇬🇧 UK → Student Finance (loans + maintenance), university bursaries
- 🇩🇪 Germany → BAföG (half grant), Deutschlandstipendium
- 🇨🇦 Canada → Canada Student Grants/Loans + provincial aid
- Everywhere → **your university's own bursary/hardship funds** — often under-claimed, ask the student office.

> 💡 University hardship & bursary funds are the least competitive money that exists — institutions want to distribute them but students don't apply.
