# 🧠 Study tools, productivity & useful websites

## Study & productivity stack

| Need | Free tool | Link |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐ Flashcards | **Anki** — spaced repetition, the med-school standard | [apps.ankiweb.net](https://apps.ankiweb.net) |

| Math & graphs | **GeoGebra** · **Desmos** · **Wolfram Alpha** (free tier) | [geogebra.org](https://www.geogebra.org) · [desmos.com](https://www.desmos.com) · [wolframalpha.com](https://www.wolframalpha.com) |
| Diagrams | **Excalidraw** · **draw.io** | [excalidraw.com](https://excalidraw.com) · [drawio.com](https://www.drawio.com) |
| Focus | **Cold Turkey** free tier · Pomodoro timers · **HazeOver** via the Pack | [getcoldturkey.com](https://getcoldturkey.com) |
| Tasks/calendar | **Todoist** free · **TickTick** free · **Trello** free · Google Calendar | [todoist.com](https://todoist.com) · [trello.com](https://trello.com) |
| File transfer | **LocalSend** · **PairDrop** (AirDrop alternatives, cross-platform) | [localsend.app](https://localsend.app) · [pairdrop.net](https://pairdrop.net) |
| Scan | **Microsoft Lens** · phone-native document scan | [microsoft.com](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/microsoft-lens) |
