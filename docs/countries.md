## 🌐 Other countries

<details>
<summary><b>Expand — general tips</b></summary>

This list started in Europe — **help us map the rest of the world!** Almost everywhere, look for:

- **The verification platforms**: SheerID, UNiDAYS, Student Beans and ISIC operate globally — most brand discounts route through them regardless of country.
- **The GitHub Student Developer Pack**: available worldwide → [github-pack.md](github-pack.md).
- **National student cards**: many countries have one (ISIC is the fallback).
- **Streaming student plans**: Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube student tiers exist in most markets — check your local site.
- **Campus licensing**: ask your IT department — Microsoft 365, MATLAB, Adobe and Autodesk are often covered institution-wide.
- **Government aid**: search `<your country> student aid / scholarship / housing aid` — or start in [scholarships.md](scholarships.md).
