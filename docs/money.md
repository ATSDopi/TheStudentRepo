## ⚠️ Money traps for students

- **"Free" trials that auto-renew** — set a cancel reminder the day you subscribe.
- **Credit cards marketed at students** — free accounts exist; don't pay fees for a card.
- **Paying for what your school already includes** — check your portal before buying software, courses, or news subscriptions.
- **Scholarship "application fees"** — scams. Real scholarships are always free to apply for.
